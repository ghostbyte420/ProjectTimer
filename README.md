## ProjectTimer </br>
```A Way to Record All Time Spend Doing Projects or Playing Games On Your PC```</br>

---
This stopwatch tool just allows users to record the time spent completing projects on their computers. I created this because </br>
I haven't kept track and sometimes I lose hundreds of hours without realizing it. This puts perspective on your time and gives you </br>
a tool you can hone your time management skills on.
